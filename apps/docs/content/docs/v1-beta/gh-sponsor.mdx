## Sponsor the project

If you find OUITRANSFER useful, consider sponsoring its development via [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/slvnlrt).

Your support helps cover infrastructure costs and funds ongoing development.

### How to sponsor

1. Go to the [OUITRANSFER repository](https://github.com/slvnlrt/ouitransfer)
2. Click the **Sponsor** button at the top of the page

![Sponsor Button](/assets/v1/sponsor/sponsor-btn.png)

3. Choose an amount (starting at $1) and billing frequency (monthly or one-time)

![Sponsor Page](/assets/v1/sponsor/sponsor-page.png)

4. Complete the payment through GitHub's secure checkout

That's it. Thank you for supporting open source development.
