Shares are the primary way to send files in Ouitransfer. Select files from your file manager, configure sharing options, and send the link to your recipients. Each share gets a unique URL and optional QR code.

For a faster, drag-and-drop alternative, see the [Quick Share](/docs/v1-beta/quick-share) widget.

To receive files from external people instead, see [Reverse Shares](/docs/v1-beta/reverse-shares).

---

## Creating a share

<Steps>
<Step>

### Select files

Navigate to the **file manager** and select the files and folders you want to share using the checkboxes, then click the **Share** button in the toolbar. This opens the share creation dialog.

</Step>
<Step>

### Configure the share

Fill in the share details:

| Option | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Yes | A label for the share. Free text. |
| **Description** | No | An optional description shown to recipients on the share page. |
| **Password** | No | Toggle on to require a password before accessing the share. |
| **Expiration** | No | A date (and optional time) after which the share becomes inaccessible. If only a date is selected, it expires at the end of that day (23:59:59). |
| **Max views** | No | Maximum number of times the share page can be accessed before it becomes unavailable. |

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm file selection

Switch to the **Files** tab to review and adjust your selection. You can search, check, and uncheck files and folders before finalizing.

</Step>
<Step>

### Share

Click **Create**. You receive a shareable link and a QR code. Copy the link or download the QR code as PNG.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Managing a share

Click on a share from your **My Shares** list to open the share details panel. From here you can:

- **View statistics**: total views, file count, recipient count
- **Edit name and description** inline (click the text to edit)
- **Copy the share link** or scan the QR code
- **Change the expiration**: toggle on/off, set a new date
- **Update password protection**: add, change, or remove the password
- **Manage files**: add or remove files and folders
- **Add recipients**: send invitation emails (see below)
- **View activity**: track who accessed and downloaded the share (see below)

### Share alias

Every share gets a short alias that produces a clean, human-readable URL (`/s/my-alias`). The alias is auto-generated from the share name but can be customized.

## Recipients and invitations

You can add email recipients to a share. Each recipient receives an invitation email with a personalized link that points to the share.

<Callout type="info">
  Sending invitation emails requires SMTP to be configured. See [Configuring SMTP](/docs/v1-beta/configuring-smtp).
</Callout>

### Adding recipients

In the share details panel, use the **Recipients** section to add email addresses. You can provide an optional name alongside each email address.

### Personalized tracking links

Each recipient receives a unique link containing a **tracking token**. When a recipient opens the share through their personalized link, Ouitransfer automatically identifies them; no additional action is required from the visitor.

This means you can see in the [activity tab](#activity-tracking) exactly which recipient accessed the share and how many times, without requiring them to fill in any form.

### Sending notifications

Once recipients are added, click **Notify** to send the invitation email. The email contains the share name, a download button with the personalized link, and the sender's name. Recipients are notified in a single batch.

### Download status per recipient

Each recipient in the list shows an explicit status badge:

- **Downloaded** (green): the recipient has downloaded at least one file from the share. A tooltip shows the date of the last download.
- **Pending** (muted): the recipient was notified but has not downloaded anything yet.

This status is distinct from the **views** count (the number of times the share page was opened). A recipient can open the share several times without downloading any file, so "views" and "Downloaded" track different things.

<Callout type="info">
  The download status is a **comfort feature, not a security control**. When a recipient arrives through their personalized email link, their identity is **verified** (it comes from a unique tracking token). When a visitor instead types a name or email into the identification form that happens to match a recipient, the attribution is **self-declared** and unverified (a subtle hint is shown in the activity view in that case). A self-declared identity could be spoofed, so do not treat "Downloaded" as proof of who downloaded.
</Callout>

### Reminding non-downloaders

When some recipients have not downloaded yet, you can send them a reminder. A **Remind (N)** button (where N is the number of pending recipients) sends a fresh reminder email (using each recipient's personalized link) only to recipients who were notified but have **not** downloaded anything. Recipients who already downloaded are skipped.

Reminders are **manual and creator-triggered**. There is no automatic scheduler: Ouitransfer never sends reminders on its own. The button is disabled when there are no pending recipients.

<Callout type="info">
  Sending reminders requires SMTP to be configured. The reminder uses a dedicated, non-configurable email type and is recorded in the [activity log](/docs/v1-beta/activity-log).
</Callout>

## Visitor identification

Ouitransfer offers four mechanisms to identify who accesses a share, from most to least precise:

### 1. Tracking tokens (automatic)

When a share has **recipients with email addresses**, each recipient's invitation link contains a unique tracking token. If a visitor arrives via one of these links, they are identified automatically with no friction.

### 2. Identification form

Shares can require visitors to identify themselves before accessing the content. Two fields can be configured independently:

| Field | Options | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Hidden / Optional / Required | Whether the visitor's name is collected |
| **Email** | Hidden / Optional / Required | Whether the visitor's email is collected |

When at least one field is set to **Optional** or **Required**, visitors see an identification dialog before accessing the share. Required fields must be filled to proceed; optional fields can be skipped.

The dialog also displays a short **privacy notice** informing visitors that their accesses and downloads may be visible to the sender of the share, so they are aware their activity is tracked before they identify themselves.

<Callout type="info">
  If a visitor has already been identified via a tracking token (from a recipient invitation link), the identification form is not shown; the token takes priority.
</Callout>

### 3. Authenticated Ouitransfer user

When a logged-in, non-owner Ouitransfer user accesses a share without a tracking token and without a matched identification-form entry, they are identified by their account. This is a **verified** attribution: it comes from their authenticated session, not a self-declared value. It is represented as the `authenticated_user` identification source in the activity view.

The precedence order (first match wins) is: tracking token → self-declared form match → authenticated account → anonymous.

### 4. Anonymous access

When none of the above apply, the visitor is recorded as **anonymous**. Only their IP address and user agent are captured for audit purposes.

### Setting identification requirements

For regular shares, visitor identification fields (`nameFieldRequired`, `emailFieldRequired`) can be set when creating or updating a share via the [API](/docs/v1-beta/api) (`POST /shares` or `PUT /shares`). Both fields accept the values `HIDDEN`, `OPTIONAL`, or `REQUIRED` and default to `HIDDEN`.

<Callout type="warn">
  The share creation dialog in the web UI does not currently expose these fields. Use the API to configure visitor identification on regular shares. For [reverse shares](/docs/v1-beta/reverse-shares), the creation form includes a dedicated **Visitor Identification** section.
</Callout>

## Activity tracking

Share owners can monitor who accesses and downloads their shared files through the **Activity** tab in the share details panel.

### What is tracked

Each visit records:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Action** | `Access` (share page loaded) or `Download` (file downloaded) |
| **Visitor identity** | Name and/or email of the visitor, or "Anonymous" |
| **Identification source** | How the visitor was identified: `tracking_token` (verified, arrived via a personalized link), `cookie` (self-declared from the identification form), `authenticated_user` (verified: a logged-in Ouitransfer account), or `anonymous` |
| **Recipient** | If the visitor was linked to a recipient, the recipient email is shown alongside the source so you can tell a verified attribution from a self-declared one |
| **Timestamp** | When the event occurred |
| **isOwner** | Whether the visit was attributed to the share's own creator (rarely true in practice, as routine owner accesses are not tracked) |

<Callout type="info">
  Both `tracking_token` and `authenticated_user` sources are verified. A `cookie` source means the visitor typed an identity into the form (or matched a recipient by self-declared email); it is convenient but unverified: a self-declared identity could be spoofed and should not be relied on as proof of identity.
</Callout>

### Filters

The activity tab provides two filter dropdowns:

- **Action**: show all events, only accesses, or only downloads
- **Identity**: show all visitors, only identified visitors, or only anonymous visitors

Results are paginated (10 per page).

<Callout type="info">
  Activity tracking is visible only to the share **owner**. Other users with the share link do not see the activity tab.
</Callout>

## Per-share notifications

In addition to your [global notification preferences](/docs/v1-beta/notification-preferences), you can enable notifications for specific shares:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Notify on each download** | Receive an email every time someone downloads a file from this share. Overrides your global `Share Download Notifications` preference for this specific share. |
| **Inactivity alert** | Receive an email if the share has not been downloaded for a specified number of days. Triggers the `Share No Activity Alert` notification. |

These settings are visible in the **Privacy** summary of the share details panel.

<Callout type="info">
  Per-share notifications require SMTP and are subject to cooldown periods to avoid excessive emails. Download and access notifications have a 15-minute cooldown per share.
</Callout>

## Share expiration and lifecycle

Shares can expire based on two conditions:

- **Expiration date**: the share becomes inaccessible after the configured date. Visitors see an "expired" message.
- **Max views**: the share becomes inaccessible after the configured number of accesses.

When a share is about to expire (or has expired), the owner receives a notification if enabled in their [notification preferences](/docs/v1-beta/notification-preferences).

Expired shares are not automatically deleted; they remain in the owner's share list and can be reactivated by extending the expiration date or increasing the view limit.
