Ouitransfer can send email notifications for a variety of events: share access, file downloads, quota warnings, and more. The Notification Preferences page lets you control which notifications you receive.

<Callout type="info">
  Email notifications require [SMTP to be configured](/docs/v1-beta/configuring-smtp) by an administrator. If SMTP is not enabled, no notifications are sent regardless of your preferences.
</Callout>

---

## Accessing preferences

Navigate to **Settings** (click your profile picture in the header) then select **Notification Preferences**. The page groups notification types by category, each with a frequency dropdown.

---

## Configurable notifications

The following notification types can be individually enabled or disabled. The frequency options are **Immediate** (send as soon as the event occurs) and **Disabled** (never send).

### Shares

| Notification | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Share Access Notifications** | Disabled | Someone viewed your share page |
| **Share Download Notifications** | Disabled | Someone downloaded a file from your share |
| **Share Expiring Soon** | Immediate | Your share is approaching its expiration date |
| **Share Expired** | Immediate | Your share has expired |
| **Share Max Views Reached** | Immediate | Your share reached its maximum view count |
| **Share No Activity Alert** | Immediate | No downloads on a share for the configured inactivity period |

### Reverse Shares

| Notification | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Reverse Share Upload Notifications** | Disabled | Someone uploaded a file to your reverse share |
| **Reverse Share Expiring Soon** | Immediate | Your reverse share is approaching its expiration date |
| **Reverse Share Expired** | Immediate | Your reverse share has expired |

### Quota

| Notification | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Storage Quota Warning** | Immediate | Your storage usage is approaching the limit |
| **Storage Quota Exceeded** | Immediate | Your storage usage has exceeded the limit |
| **Files Auto-Deleted** | Immediate | Files were automatically deleted (e.g., expired share cleanup) |
| **Share Auto-Deleted** | Immediate | A share was automatically deleted |

### Admin

These notifications are only relevant for administrator accounts.

| Notification | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **New User Registration** | Immediate | A new user account was created on the platform |
| **User Quota Alert** | Immediate | A user's storage quota has reached a warning or critical threshold |

<Callout type="info">
  **Share Access** and **Share Download** notifications default to **Disabled** because they can generate a high volume of emails on popular shares. Enable them selectively if you need to track access on specific shares — or use the per-share notification overrides described below.
</Callout>

## Non-configurable notifications

Some notifications are critical for account operation and are always sent. They do not appear on the preferences page and cannot be disabled:

| Notification | Description |
|---|---|
| **Welcome** | Sent when your account is created |
| **Password Reset** | Sent when you request a password reset |
| **Account Deactivated** | Sent when your account is deactivated by an admin |
| **Account Reactivated** | Sent when your account is reactivated |
| **Share Invitations** | Sent to recipients when they are invited to a share |
| **Reverse Share Invitations** | Sent to recipients of a reverse share invitation |
| **Test Email** | Sent by admins to verify SMTP configuration |

## Per-share overrides

Individual shares and reverse shares can override your global preferences:

- On a [share](/docs/v1-beta/shares), the **Notify on each download** toggle enables download notifications for that specific share, even if your global **Share Download Notifications** preference is disabled.
- On a [share](/docs/v1-beta/shares), the **Inactivity alert** setting triggers a notification after a configured number of days without downloads, using the **Share No Activity Alert** type.
- On a [reverse share](/docs/v1-beta/reverse-shares), the **Notify on upload** toggle enables upload notifications for that specific reverse share, even if your global **Reverse Share Upload Notifications** preference is disabled.

<Callout type="warn">
  If a notification type is **Disabled** in your global preferences, per-share overrides can still trigger notifications for that type. Per-share settings act as explicit opt-ins for individual shares.
</Callout>

## Cooldown periods

To prevent excessive emails on busy shares, certain notification types enforce a cooldown:

| Notification | Cooldown |
|---|---|
| Share Access Notifications | 15 minutes per share |
| Share Download Notifications | 15 minutes per share |
| Reverse Share Upload Notifications | 5 minutes per reverse share |

During the cooldown window, additional events of the same type on the same share are silently skipped — they are not queued or batched for later delivery.

---

## Unsubscribing

Every non-critical notification email includes an **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom. Clicking it opens a confirmation page where you can disable that notification type with a single click.

This is equivalent to setting the notification to **Disabled** on your preferences page. You can re-enable it at any time from Settings.

## API reference

| Method | Path | Auth | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/notifications/preferences` | User | Get all notification preferences (merged with catalog defaults) |
| `PUT` | `/notifications/preferences` | User | Update notification preferences (batch) |
| `GET` | `/notifications/unsubscribe?token=...` | — | Validate unsubscribe token and return notification type |
| `POST` | `/notifications/unsubscribe` | — | Confirm unsubscribe (sets frequency to `disabled`) |

The `GET /notifications/preferences` endpoint returns all 22 notification types with their current frequency, whether they are configurable, and whether they are critical. The `PUT` endpoint accepts an array of `{ type, frequency }` objects — only configurable, non-critical types can be updated.

For the full API reference, see the [API documentation](/docs/v1-beta/api).
