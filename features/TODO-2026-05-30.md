## Pre-existing bugs found and skipped

### ~~B-26~~ [FIXED] — `updateShare` clears expiration when not included in payload

- **File**: `apps/server/src/modules/share/service.ts:413`
- **Description**: `expiration: shareData.expiration ? new Date(shareData.expiration) : null` — if `shareData.expiration` is `undefined` (client didn't include it), this sets expiration to `null`, silently clearing it.
- **Fix**: Destructured `expiration` from `shareData` spread, conditional spread into `updateData` only when `expiration !== undefined`.
- **Status**: FIXED — committed `fix(server): preserve expiration when not in updateShare payload (B-26)`
