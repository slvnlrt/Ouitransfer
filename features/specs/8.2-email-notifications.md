# 8.2 — Email Notifications

**Status:** Not started
**Depends on:** — (SMTP settings already exist)

## Summary

Comprehensive email notification system covering account lifecycle, share events,
and configurable user preferences.

## Scope

### Account Lifecycle
- Account creation welcome email
- Password reset email (flow may already exist — verify current state)

### Share Notifications
- **Invite mode** for shares and reverse-shares: instead of just generating a URL,
  optionally specify recipient email(s) / name(s) and send invitation emails
  - Each invitee gets a personal link with unique access credentials
  - Enables download/upload tracking per invitee (see 8.3)
- Share password included in invitation email (when applicable)
- Share expiration reminder (e.g., "your share expires in 24h")

### Activity Notifications (per-share settings)
- Download notification: email when someone downloads from your share
- Upload notification: email when someone uploads to your reverse share
- Inactivity alert: email when nobody has downloaded after N days (configurable)

### Service / Maintenance Emails
- Quota warning: daily reminder when user is near/over quota
- Share expired: notification when a share auto-expires
- Files auto-deleted: notification when cleanup removes old files

### User Preferences
- Global notification preferences (per-account settings page)
- Per-share notification toggle (on the share creation/details modal)
- Email frequency: immediate, daily digest, or disabled

### SMTP Test
- "Send test email" button in Settings > Email (with recipient address input)
- Currently only "Test connection" exists — add actual email delivery test
